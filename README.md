# ☕ Afficionado Coffee Roasters
## Product Optimization & Revenue Contribution Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built with Streamlit to analyze
product performance, revenue contribution, and menu optimization opportunities
for Afficionado Coffee Roasters.

---

## 🔗 Live Dashboard
[Click here to open the dashboard](https://your-streamlit-url.streamlit.app)

---

## 📊 Features

- **Product Ranking** — Top & bottom products by sales volume and revenue
- **Revenue Contribution** — Revenue share % per product and category
- **Pareto Analysis** — 80/20 rule to identify revenue anchors & long-tail products
- **Quadrant Analysis** — Hero, Volume Driver, Premium Niche & Underperformer classification
- **Store Comparison** — Revenue heatmap across all 3 store locations
- **Interactive Filters** — Filter by category, product type, store & top-N products
